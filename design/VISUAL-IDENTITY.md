# TaskForge Visual Identity & Asset Guidelines

**Status:** Initial identity direction  
**Relationship:** Visual identity sits on top of `DESIGN-FOUNDATION.md`; it must not override accessibility or semantic-color rules.

## 1. Brand idea

**TaskForge** represents structured work being shaped into an outcome.

The mark combines:

- an open task/document form;
- a strong structural joint;
- a subtle `T`;
- an open edge suggesting extension rather than a closed monolith.

The identity should feel engineered and precise, not industrial-themed or game-like.

## 2. Name

Official spelling: **TaskForge**

Do not use `Task Forge`, `TASKFORGE` or `TaskFORGE` as the primary product wordmark.

## 3. Mark

Primary asset: `assets/brand/taskforge-mark.svg`.

The mark may appear without the wordmark in:

- favicon;
- app rail;
- repository avatar;
- launcher/app icon;
- compact loading/identity surfaces.

Do not place letters `TF` inside a generic rounded square as the primary mark.

## 4. Brand color

Primary forge orange:

- `#D8610B` — normal brand mark/accent
- `#A94400` — stronger/darker brand treatment
- `#18181B` — graphite
- `#FFFFFF` — white

Orange is a brand color, not the warning status color.

## 5. Clear space

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least 25% of the mark's width.

Do not crowd the mark against navigation edges, text, badges or container borders.

## 6. Minimum size

- Favicon: use the favicon-specific simplified asset.
- General UI: do not render the full mark below 20×20 px.
- App rail: 24–32 px visual mark is preferred.
- Wordmark lockup: do not use when the mark would be smaller than 24 px.

## 7. Backgrounds

Preferred:

1. orange mark on white;
2. orange mark on graphite;
3. graphite mark on white;
4. white mark on graphite.

Avoid placing the mark on noisy images or semantic status colors.

## 8. Favicon

The favicon uses only the mark. It intentionally removes fine detail and increases stroke weight at small sizes.

Provided assets:

- `favicon.svg`
- `favicon-16x16.png`
- `favicon-32x32.png`
- `apple-touch-icon.png`
- `icon-192.png`
- `icon-512.png`

## 9. Voice

TaskForge copy is concise, factual and operational.

Preferred:
- `No tasks match this view.`
- `Permission removed.`
- `3 tasks could not be updated.`
- `Why can't I close this task?`

Avoid:
- exaggerated productivity claims;
- cute empty-state copy;
- vague enterprise language;
- jargon where a concrete action is available.

## 10. Relationship to Casual Office

TaskForge should share foundational interaction quality and reusable primitives with Casual Office while retaining a distinct product identity. Brand-specific assets and product patterns belong in TaskForge; generic controls belong in the shared design system.
